How it works

Three taps. No app.

01

Scan & join

Guests scan a QR code at the venue and join in seconds with their name and phone or email.

02

Add to Wallet

Their rewards card goes straight into Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, on iPhone and Android alike.

03

Earn & enjoy

Points are added on every visit. The card updates by itself, and rewards are ready when they are earned.

For venues

Built for premium hospitality.

Staff app

Scan a guest's card, add points from the bill and hand out rewards, from any phone or tablet.

Owner dashboard

Members, visits, top guests and rewards given, live. Set your own points rules and rewards.

Your brand

The card carries your name and style, and you can send offers straight to your guests' lock screens.

Support & policies

Help with your card.

Customer support

For questions about your points or rewards, ask the staff at the venue where you joined, or contact us:

  • Email: support email coming soon
  • Location: Kingdom of Bahrain

To remove your card, delete it from your Wallet. To have your details deleted, contact us or the venue.

Privacy

  • We keep only your name and your phone number or email, to run your rewards card.
  • We never sell your details.
  • The venue owns its members' data; Loyalty processes it on its behalf.
  • You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time.
  • Points have no cash value. Rewards are set by each venue and may change.
